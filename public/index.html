The driver

Luke

Luke drives in Club100, the arrive-and-drive championship where every kart is prepared and allocated by the organisers, so results come down to the driver. For 2027 he is registered for both Junior championships and is entered for every round.

The season runs from the January opener to the Silverstone finale in November, taking in circuits from Rowrah in Cumbria to Llandow in Wales, with the Rotax Racing World Finals at South Garda Karting, Lonato, in September.
